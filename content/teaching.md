## Algorithm

- **Institution**: IMT Atlantique
- **Years dispensed**: 2024 -- 2025
- **Details**: Designed for first-year engineering students, this module serves primarily as a refresher. It consolidates their understanding of algorithmic concepts, covering key topics such as complexity, recursion, and sorting.

## Concurrent computing

- **Institution**: IMT Atlantique
- **Years dispensed**: 2023 -- 2024, 2024 -- 2025
- **Details**: This module focuses on the principles of concurrent computing and their practical application. Students first familiarise themselves with core concepts and [FSP notation](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~jnm/LTSdocumention/FSP-notation.html) before collaborating on a system modelling project. Finally, working in pairs, they develop a Java-based simulation of a railway network.

## Collaborative software development

- **Institution**: IMT Atlantique
- **Years dispensed**: 2024 -- 2025
- **Details**: I delivered the first part of this module, introducing students to the fundamentals of operating systems and terminal commands.
- **Note**: Alongside teaching, I helped design course materials by authoring new content and translating instructional resources.

## Engineering study

- **Institution**: IMT Mines Alès
- **Years dispensed**: 2024 -- 2025, 2025 -- 2026
- **Context**: The engineering study is a collaborative exercise where teams of two to three students respond to a technical brief from an academic or industrial partner. As a supervisor, I define the project's scope and guide the students towards achieving their objectives.
- **Goal**: The students are required to deliver a technical solution that meets strict specifications within a tight timeframe.

**2025 -- 2026**: *Study of digital twin development practices in open-source projects.*

Based on the work of the previous about the exploration of digital twins open-source repositories, students choose to improve the methodology to find DTs on GitHub's public repositories. To do so, they implemented a new process leveraging LLMs and shell programs.

**2024 -- 2025**: *DevOps for digital twins.*

In this study, the students explored ways to find public repositories containing digital twins and create an initial dataset of DTs. They generated 300+ DT-related keywords using LLMs and used them as inputs against GitHub and Software Heritage's REST API. They found around 90,000 corresponding repositories and analysed their metadata.

## R&D mission

- **Institution**: IMT Mines Alès
- **Years dispensed**: 2023 -- 2024
- **Context**: The R&D mission is a collaborative exercise where groups of two to three students respond to a specific brief from an academic or industrial partner. As a supervisor, I define the project scope and oversee the students to ensure they meet their objectives.
- **Goal**: This project introduces students to the research environment. They are expected to deliver a brief state-of-the-art review alongside the preliminary implementation of a technical solution.

**2023 -- 2024**: *DevOps for digital twins.*

In this mission, the students provided a brief overview on DevOps techniques applied to digital twins.
